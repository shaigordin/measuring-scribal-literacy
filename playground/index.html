Data: TLHdig Beta 0.3, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20328284. The ten reference traditions are defined from the literature: the Hurrian groups of Gordin, Yavasan & Romach 2025 (StBoT 73, Table 3), the Luwian, Ḫuwaššanna and Palaic festivals as the HFR edition modules divide them, Kizzuwatna rituals (Miller 2004 and others), the Luwian rituals of Puriyanni and Kuwattalla (Yakubovich & Mouton 2023), Arzawa / western rituals (Bawanypeck 2005, Mouton 2010) and the Hattian cult layer (Klinger 1996). A fragment is assigned only when its nearest tradition beats the runner-up by at least the abstention margin, calibrated on held-out windows to about 80% precision. The open-set check flags a fragment that fits none of them: either its best similarity is below what 95% of reference fragments of its size reach, or it sits nearest to a genre outside the reference (myths, prayers, oracles, treaties, instructions, cult inventories, festivals of other traditions, the Luwian rituals of Taurisa). Everything runs in your browser; nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
