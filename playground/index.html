How far the method gets

Texts of five or more legible words are judged; shorter ones are counted but not judged. "Placed" means the nearest tradition wins by the abstention margin and the open-set check finds nothing against it. Reference manuscripts are judged held out, without themselves in their tradition.

Data: TLHdig Beta 0.3, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20328284. CTH bins and titles as TLHdig and the Konkordanz der hethitischen Keilschrifttafeln (S. Košak, hethport.net) give them. The ten reference traditions, the abstention margin and the open-set check are explained on Place a fragment. Everything runs in your browser.
